This domain is for businesses whose staff use AI assistants or AI features in their software, businesses that build AI into products, and employers using automated hiring tools.

Existing regulators apply existing laws to AI. New York City enforces Local Law 144, and the EU AI Act applies to AI used in the EU.

- [20 minutes to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework](20-minutes-to-nist-ai-rmf.md): about 10 minutes.
- [20 minutes to NYC Local Law 144](20-minutes-to-nyc-local-law-144.md): about 5 minutes.
- [20-day learning plan: AI use](20-days-to-comply.md): 20 working days.
