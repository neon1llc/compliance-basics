This domain is for any business that takes credit or debit cards, and service providers that handle card data or can affect its security.

Card brands and acquirers enforce PCI DSS through merchant agreements. The PCI Security Standards Council publishes it.

- [20 minutes to PCI DSS](20-minutes-to-pci-dss.md): about 10 minutes.
- [20-day learning plan: Payments](20-days-to-comply.md): 20 working days.
