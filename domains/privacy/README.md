This domain is for every business that keeps personal information about customers, clients, or employees, especially those with customers in several states.

State attorneys general enforce breach and privacy laws, California also has a privacy agency, and the FTC enforces Section 5 of the FTC Act.

- [20 minutes to state privacy laws](20-minutes-to-state-privacy-laws.md): about 10 minutes.
- [20-day learning plan: Privacy](20-days-to-comply.md): 20 working days.
